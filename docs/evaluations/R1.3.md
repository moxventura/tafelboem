# R1.3 "Dekking": evaluation

Release 1.3.0. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-tafelboem-design.md`, *R1.3 "Dekking"*.
Built before playtest 4 by the parent's choice; if playtest 4 says building isn't fun, R1.3 is
reconsidered before it is published.

## Hypothesis

Dodging and traps make the build count in the fight, without cutting answers per minute or adding
frustration. Dodging, lying bombs and traps ship together; the fight line counts each apart
(`dodged`, `picked up`, `trapped`).

## Go when

- answers per minute in the fight are no lower than playtest 4's, and % right and "?" are no worse
- the kids dodge and build traps without being told
- no tears from deaths or from bombs piling up
- the kids still ask to play

## Playtest 5

Not played yet. Numbers from the `TafelBoem fight:`, `yeet:`, `build:` and `session:` lines. Watch:

- bunker play: many dodges, few pick-ups
- bombs piling up (there is no cap)
- whether traps change what they build (the build line shows cactus, cobweb, magma placed)
- how many arena deaths (`died`), and on which difficulty they play
- the pull: does a yeet bending into a trap look like his flight, or like a jump?
- A bomb lands within 2 blocks of a kid who stands still, with only about 0.1 block to spare on
  diagonal throws: does a kid who fidgets get "dodged" and miss sums?
- The building chest and bell stop bombs like any build: a kid behind them is hard to hit.
- On LAN, when both kids answer at once, a yeet can be credited to the other sibling (one shared
  kick).
- A bomb thrown at a kid who is busy or gone counts as dodged and he says "Mis!".
- The trap's hop can add up to about 4 blocks to a yeet's distance and record.
- A capped hit on Easy counts as a miss for the attacker: arrows bounce off a kid at low health.
- Check by hand once: on Easy, a fall from a tall build and a mob hit in an arena leave half a
  heart (the real damage hook isn't covered by GameTests); hitting or right-clicking a lying bomb
  starts its question.
