# R1.4 "Terug!": evaluation

Release 1.4.0. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-tafelboem-design.md`, *R1.4 "Terug!"*.
Decided with the parent on 2026-11-13, after the playtest of 1.3.0 (`R1.2.md`, `R1.3.md`). The kids
didn't like building; dodging was too easy, and the bombs left lying around were no fun.

## Hypothesis

Dodging that is hard but pays off with a throw-back makes the fight more fun and gives more sums per
minute, not fewer.

Learning rule: a dodge never takes health off Graaf Fout by itself. It earns an extra chance at a
hit, and that hit still needs a right answer.

## Go when

- dodged throws at a free kid, both kids pooled, are 20–45% on level 1 and 10–30% on level 3
  (`dodged` over `thrown at` in the fight lines, per `level`; the `TafelBoem throw:` lines have the
  distance, the flight and the kid's speed behind each)
- at least 4 answers per minute in the fight (playtest 3 on 1.1.1 reached 3.9 and 4.7), and % right and "?" no worse than playtest 3
- the kids catch and throw back without being told, and at least some throw-backs hit
- the kids still ask to play

## Watch

- how often the kids juggle, and whether they try to (`juggled` in the fight line)
- a kid who hoards bombs instead of throwing (`caught` against `threw back`)
- one sibling catching most of the bombs
- throw-backs that keep missing: is the aim help enough? (`threw back` against `hit`; the spec's aim
  is that the younger kid hits a standing Graaf Fout at least half the time). The homing catches a
  throw up to just under 5 blocks off-aim (the spec says 5: a straight throw 5 blocks beside him never
  comes within the 5-block homing range of his middle)
- whether the ring makes it too easy (it has a switch, `landingRing`)
- deaths on Normal (`died`)
- whether adventure mode in the fight bothers them (`noBuildInFight`)
- the ring is drawn at 2.8 blocks and the zone is 3.0: a kid standing between the two is hit
  although outside the ring; does it look like cheating?
- the yeet cap by room (`YeetPower.capForRoom`): a throw-back or super yeet gets the power whose flight
  fits the floor ahead, less 2 blocks; when away from the kid is too short, he flies through the
  middle to the far side instead. From the middle of a level 3 arena that is about 20 blocks (a
  Kippenbom's throw-back), so a Gewone TNT's and a Kippenbom's throw-back and super yeet often fly the
  same, and a plain kick (never capped) can still fly further or out of the arena. From near a wall,
  through the middle, there is room for up to about 40. Do they feel like a reward next to a kick? Does
  flying back over the kid's head, through the middle, look right?
- two kids at once: a juggle freezes every timer in the fight, including the other kid's questions' pause;
  a sibling's right answer while he hangs sends him off and closes the juggle question unanswered

## Check by hand once

Things the GameTests can't see: right-clicking a Terugbom in a real client throws it; Q does
nothing; a Terugbom dragged into a chest comes back; the hint appears once; nobody can build in a
fight and everybody can again after it; the ring and the glow look right in daylight.
