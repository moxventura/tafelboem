# R1.2 "Bouwen": evaluation

Release 1.2.0. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-tafelboem-design.md`, *R1.2 "Bouwen"*.

## Hypothesis

Building first makes a round more fun, without cutting answers per minute in the fight below
playtest 3 (Kid A 4.7, Kid B 3.9; inside the fights 5.0 and 4.0).

1.1.1 (camera nudge, P29) and 1.1.2 (ready-made world P26, leave message P28, fewer dizzy spells
P24) reach the kids in the same playtest; their effects can't be told apart from R1.2's.

## Go when

- each kid says building was fun and wants it again next time
- answers per minute in the fight, from his arrival, are no lower than playtest 3's (Kid A 5.0,
  Kid B 4.0 inside the fights), and % right and "?" are no worse
- the build lines show they built, and the bell or the time ran out without frustration
- the kids still ask to play

## Playtest 4

Not played yet. Numbers from the `TafelBoem build:`, `fight:` and `session:` lines; the parent asks
each kid *"Was bouwen leuk? Wil je het de volgende keer weer?"* and checks P24, P26, P28 and P29.

Also watch for:

- Graaf Fout now appears on top of whatever they built in the middle; does that look right?
- Blocks above the 48-block slot or over the arena's outer edge stay in the world; do they bother
  anyone?
- A kid who falls from a tall build in survival can die, respawns in the hub and has left the run
  (no fall-damage change in R1.2).
- A kid who joins, or is caught from the void, lands on the arrival spot even if someone built
  there.
- A kid rings the bell by accident (it stands 2 blocks from the chest).
