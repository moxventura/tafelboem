# R1.1 evaluation: "Meer yeets"

**Hypothesis:** more variety keeps them coming back.

The kids play R1 and R1.1 for the first time in the same session (Jesper chose to build R1.1 before
playtest 3), so this session also fills in the numbers of [`R1.md`](R1.md). R1's and R1.1's effects
can't be told apart; the `TafelBoem yeet:` lines show what R1.1 did. The baseline is R0.1: about 5
answers per minute.

- **Date:** to fill in
- **Version:** to fill in (from the mod list in `latest.log`; the first R1.1 build is 1.1.0)
- **Who played:** to fill in (names and ages)
- **PCs:** the woonkamer PC and a second PC over LAN
- **World:** a TafelBoem world (an R1 world works too)

**Sources.** The host PC's `latest.log` has, from 1.1.0 on:
- `TafelBoem config:` once per world: which switches were on
- `TafelBoem yeet:` per kick: who, the bomb and the sum, the answer time and the usual time, fast,
  the streak, the factor, the flight and the landing (and whether it was the bomb's own), how far,
  out of the arena, and a record with the best before it
- `TafelBoem chickens:` per counting Kippenbom: the sum and the groups
- `TafelBoem fight:` and `TafelBoem session:` as in R1; the fight line ends with the farthest yeet

**Watch for** (write down when and where):
- Graaf Fout hanging still in the air: he may hang for up to 15 s and then pop back next to a kid. If
  so, note when and where, and look for a `TafelBoem stuck:` line in `latest.log`.
- How often the wall splat happens, and whether he ever flies out through a hole a crater blew in
  the wall.
- Him sticking in mid-air against a low block (a 1-block bump or a kid's build) during a wall splat.
- Running round, jittering on the spot next to a kid.
- The chase chickens not poofing when the chase ends (they should be gone with it), or nudging a kid.
- Whether the counting chickens read as groups on big sums: on 10 × 10 the groups stand close
  together in a ring and look like one crowd. Note from which sum on, and ask the kid how many
  groups they saw.
- A counting group that drops out of sight (its first chicken flew over a hole and popped while
  falling).
