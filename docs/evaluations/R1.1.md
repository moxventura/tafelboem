# R1.1 evaluation: "Meer yeets"

**Hypothesis:** more variety keeps them coming back.

The kids play R1 and R1.1 for the first time in the same session (the parent chose to build R1.1 before
playtest 3), so this session also fills in the numbers of [`R1.md`](R1.md). R1's and R1.1's effects
can't be told apart; the `TafelBoem yeet:` lines show what R1.1 did. The baseline is R0.1: about 5
answers per minute.

- **Date:** 2026-10-31 (playtest 3, together with R1)
- **Version:** 1.1.0 on both PCs, every R1.1 switch on (`TafelBoem config:` line)
- **Who played:** Kid B and Kid A (ages to fill in)
- **PCs:** two PCs, each kid alone in their own world: the LAN join failed (P22 in `R1.md`)
- **World:** a TafelBoem world (an R1 world works too)

**Sources.** The host PC's `latest.log` has, from 1.1.0 on:
- `TafelBoem config:` once per world: which switches were on

## 4. Findings

Check the cause of each finding in the code before writing it down. The numbers continue from
`R1.md`, which ended at P21.

| # | What we saw | Cause | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| P23 | The fast bonus never came: 0 of 96 kicks. Only 3 kicks had a usual time at all. | By design, but it means the boost can't show in a first session: a usual time needs 3 earlier right answers on the same sum (3 × 7 and 7 × 3 together, `AnswerTimes.usual`), and a session of 40–60 answers spread over ~50 sums rarely gets there. Kid A's world was new; Kid B's had one fight from that morning. It will start to come as they keep playing the same world. | 📏 Can't judge yet: look again after a few sessions in the same world |
| P24 | The dizzy spell is most of the landings (51 of 86) and often comes twice in a row (25 times), although check 3 says no landing twice in a row. | The "never twice in a row" only holds for the landings `ReactionPicker` picks. Boomerang and wall splat always end dizzy (29 kicks, `YeetStyle.hasOwnEnding`), and so does flying out of the arena (20 kicks, `BossFight.flewOut`). Picked landings were only 35 of 86. | 💡 Proposed: let flying out and the own endings count for "not twice in a row" (skip boomerang/wall splat right after a dizzy), or give flying out its own landing |
| P25 | Graaf Fout flew out of the arena on 25 of 86 kicks (29%), and 4 of the 12 records were out of the arena. | Anything that drops him below the arena floor counts (`BossFight.tick`); with the boost and the bombs' power the longer flights clear the walls. | 📏 Watch: ask whether flying out is fun (it ends the flight early and always lands dizzy) |
| P29 | They complained that they couldn't move the way they wanted while an animation was playing. | Two things take control away. While the sum is open, and for the 6 s correction after a wrong answer, the kid is rooted (`RootLock`). After a right answer they can walk again, but camera assist turns their view to Graaf Fout for up to 7 s while he flies (`KICK_CAM_MAX_TICKS`), and again for 1.5–2 s after he lands (`followKickerFor`). | 🐞 It was the camera turning (the parent, 2026-10-31). 1.1.1: only a short nudge at the kick and one when he lands out of view (`cameraNudge`); no check yet on whether they fight it |
