# R1 evaluation: "Kies je level"

No video: the numbers come from `latest.log` and the logs, the rest is typed by the parent.

**Hypothesis:** with a world of their own, a hub to come back to and three levels to choose from,
the kids keep asking to play, and over time they pick harder levels and collect stars.

- **Date:** playtest 3, 2026-10-31 (Kid B also played one fight at 08:41; the session was 14:04–14:21)
- **Version:** 1.1.0 on both PCs (R1 and R1.1 together, see [`R1.1.md`](R1.1.md))
- **Who played:** Kid B and Kid A (ages to fill in)
- **PCs:** two PCs, but **not over LAN**: Kid B's PC couldn't join Kid A's world (P22), so each kid
  played alone in his own world, at the same time. Every fight has `1 kid`.
- **World:** a new world with World Type: TafelBoem
- **Language:** the game ran in English ("Count Wrong"), which is fine for them

**Sources.** From 1.0.1 on, `latest.log` of the PC that hosts the world (on LAN: the one that opened
it) holds the numbers for every kid. Each fight that ends adds a `TafelBoem fight:` line (level,
arena, won/left, stars, answers, wrong, "?", ESC, seconds, kids), and each kid gets a
`TafelBoem session:` line when they leave or the world closes (minutes in the world, answers,
answers per minute from first to last answer, % right, "?", ESC, and their fights in order). Sharing
that one `latest.log` is enough; `/tafelboem stats <player>` adds the stars and re-ask accuracy.
The same numbers are also in the logs in the world folder. The fights log is
`saves/<world>/tafelboem/fights-<player>.jsonl`: one line per kid per fight, with the time, the
level, the arena, the result (`won`, `left` or `disconnected`), answers, wrong answers, "?" and ESC,
stars, seconds (of the whole run, from the first kid in, not of one kid), how many kids took part
and the version. The answer log, next to it, has every answer with its level and time. The game log
holds the chat but not the action bar, so the stars at the door and the yeet-o-meter don't show in
it. The parent types the rest.
