# R1 evaluation: "Kies je level"

No video: the numbers come from `latest.log` and the logs, the rest is typed by Jesper.

**Hypothesis:** with a world of their own, a hub to come back to and three levels to choose from,
the kids keep asking to play, and over time they pick harder levels and collect stars.

- **Date:** playtest 3, not played yet
- **Version:** to fill in (the version from the mod list in `latest.log`; the first published R1
  build is 1.0.0)
- **Who played:** to fill in (names and ages)
- **PCs:** the woonkamer PC and a second PC over LAN
- **World:** a new world with World Type: TafelBoem

**Sources.** From 1.0.1 on, `latest.log` of the PC that hosts the world (on LAN: the one that opened
it) holds the numbers for every kid. Each fight that ends adds a `TafelBoem fight:` line (level,
arena, won/left, stars, answers, wrong, "?", ESC, seconds, kids), and each kid gets a
`TafelBoem session:` line when they leave or the world closes (minutes in the world, answers,
answers per minute from first to last answer, % right, "?", ESC, and their fights in order). Sharing
that one `latest.log` is enough; `/tafelboem stats <player>` adds the stars and re-ask accuracy.
The same numbers are also in the logs in the world folder. The fights log is
`saves/<world>/tafelboem/fights-<player>.jsonl`: one line per kid per fight, with the time, the
level, the arena, the result (`won`, `left` or `disconnected`), answers, wrong answers, "?" and ESC,
stars, seconds (of the whole run, from the first kid in, not of one kid), how many kids took part
and the version. The answer log, next to it, has every answer with its level and time. The game log
holds the chat but not the action bar, so the stars at the door and the yeet-o-meter don't show in
it. Jesper types the rest.

## 4. Playtest 3 findings

Check the cause of each finding in the code before writing it down. The numbers continue from
playtest 2 (`R0.1.md`), which ended at P17.

Before playtest 3, Jesper played 1.0.1 through once himself (2026-10-15) and found P18–P21.

| # | What we saw | Cause | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| P18 | Some bombs open the space under an arena wall, and you can walk out into the void. | The plain arenas stood their obsidian wall (4 high) on the floor's dirt and grass. A blast beside the wall carves those two blocks and leaves a gap 2 high under it, which a kid fits through (`ArenaShape.build`). Fails check 5. | 🐞 Fixed in 1.0.2: obsidian under the walls down to the bedrock |
| P19 | Playing an arena a second time leaves items from the last fight on the floor. | The door clears the arena's entities, but the arena is 512+ blocks away and its chunks were saved and unloaded by then. Entities that aren't loaded can't be found, so they came back from disk when the kid arrived. A world reload did the same. | 🐞 Fixed in 1.0.2: anything but a player that loads from disk in an arena slot is a leftover and goes |
| P20 | You can't dodge Graaf Fout or attack him. | By design: the question starts wherever his bomb lands, and nothing but an answer hurts him, so a sword hit shows nothing at all. | 💡 Backlog: dodgeable bombs are the R1.2 experiment; a no-damage "only sums hurt me" reaction to a hit is proposed |
| P21 | There's no interesting battle music. | The mod played no music; vanilla's boss track only plays in the End. | 💡 Done in 1.0.2: a track per level |
